# Happy Home Tasks — תורנויות הבית

**The chore board that knows whose turn it is, so your family doesn't have to
argue about it.**

This is the product description for people who use the app. What the app does
and why it is built that way is in [HOW-IT-WORKS.md](HOW-IT-WORKS.md); how to
run it is in [README.md](README.md).

## What it does

Happy Home Tasks is a shared chore list for everyone who lives under one roof.

You add the jobs that come around again and again — dishes, bins, vacuuming,
walking the dog — and you say who takes part in each one. From then on, the app
takes over the hard part: it works out whose turn each chore is, every day, and
keeps a truthful record of who actually did what.

Everyone in the house sees the same board on their own phone, updated the moment
someone taps "done". You can add it to your phone's home screen and use it like
any other app. It's free, with no charge per person.

## Problems it solves

**No more "it's not my turn."** The app holds the running order and moves it
along by itself. Nobody has to remember, and nobody can quietly skip themselves.

**No more rewriting history.** Once a job is marked done, it belongs to the
person who did it, permanently. A holiday booked next week, or a change made
next month, can never go back and reassign the dishes you washed yesterday.

**Going away doesn't break everything.** Mark yourself as away and your turns
pass to someone else while you're gone, then come back to you when you return.
You don't have to rebuild the whole schedule around one trip.

**Missed jobs don't vanish.** If a day gets skipped, it stays on the board as
overdue and today's card tells you how long it's been waiting. Things stop
falling through the cracks silently.

**Kids without phones are included.** Add family members who don't have their
own account. A tablet in the kitchen or a parent's phone can mark their chore
done for them, and it's still credited to them by name.

**Proof, not promises.** Snap a photo when you finish a job. Everyone can react
to it and leave a comment, which turns the chore log into something the family
genuinely enjoys checking.

**An honest answer to "who does more?"** A simple chart shows who did what over
the last week, two weeks or month — based on jobs actually completed, not on who
complains loudest.

**Fair swaps, not favours forgotten.** When someone covers for someone else, or
two people trade days, the app records the trade properly so it doesn't cost
anybody an extra turn later.

## How to use it

**Step 1 — Sign in.** Open the app and sign in with your Google account.

**Step 2 — Create your home, or join one.** The first person taps *Create new
home* and gets a short house code. Everyone else signs in and types that code to
join. Whoever created the home is its manager and can make the bigger changes
described below.

**Step 3 — Add the people who live there.** Anyone with a Google account joins
themselves using the code. For a young child, or anyone without a phone, add
them from Settings as a household member and give them a photo. They'll take
their turns like everyone else.

**Step 4 — Add your chores.** For each one: give it a name, choose how often it
happens (every day, once a week, or only on days you pick), and add the people
who share it in the order you want them to take turns. You can tag it with a
room if that helps.

**Step 5 — Check the board and tap "done".** The app opens on today and shows
your jobs first; one tap switches to the whole household. There's also a week
view showing the seven days ahead and who's on each one. When you finish a job,
tap *done* — add a photo if you like — and the turn passes to the next person
automatically. Tapped it by mistake? Undo it.

**Step 6 — Tell the app when life gets in the way.** Heading away? Switch
yourself to *not here* and your turns are covered. The house manager can also
pass a turn to the next person, write off a day that genuinely doesn't need
doing, drag a job onto a different day, trade two people's days with each other,
change the running order for good, or add an extra round of something when today
needs it twice.

**Step 7 — Look back whenever you like.** The Activity tab shows the
who-did-what chart and a running feed of everything that happened, complete with
photos, reactions and comments. You can also add a note for something that was
never on the list, like clearing out the garage.

**Step 8 — Switch on reminders.** In Settings, turn on a nudge for when it's
your turn and the job isn't done yet. Worth knowing up front: these appear only
while the app is open on your phone — there are no background alerts yet.

## Who it's for

Families with children old enough to pitch in. Flatmates splitting a place.
Couples who'd like the washing-up question settled once and for all. The app is
built in Hebrew, for households that want their chore list in their own
language.
